# Venture Associate Application Website

This project is a personal application website for a Venture Associate role in Jeddah.

The goal is not to look flashy for the sake of it. The goal is to feel thoughtful, warm, sharp, and clearly venture minded.

It should communicate:

- founder proximity
- startup ecosystem fluency
- strong technical background
- real product building experience
- curiosity about startups and venture
- credibility without sounding arrogant
- warmth and human tone
- non corporate and non AI sounding writing

## Core positioning

The user is a nontraditional but strong candidate for venture.

He may not come from the classic investment banking to VC path, but he has:

- real startup and founder exposure
- strong technical background
- experience building hardware and software
- experience using AI and working on AI products
- exposure to Saudi and China startup ecosystems
- founder empathy
- ecosystem contribution through workshops, sessions, and mentoring
- strong academic and talent signals

The site should frame this as:

"I may be nontraditional on paper, but I already think, build, and operate in ways that are highly relevant to venture."

## Tone

The tone should be:

- warm
- human
- thoughtful
- confident
- not over polished
- not overly corporate
- not overly dramatic
- not AI sounding
- no hyphen heavy copy

The writing should feel like a real person who is smart, ambitious, and self aware.

## Visual direction

The visual direction should be:

- premium dark theme
- elegant, clean, modern
- subtle emerald or teal accent
- strong readability
- soft borders and glassy cards
- minimal clutter
- more investor taste than startup hackathon vibe
